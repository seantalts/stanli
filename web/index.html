stanli: full Stan in the browser

stanc3 (OCaml→JS) compiles the model, stanli.wasm lowers it to an op graph and runs NUTS. No server, no C++ toolchain; everything below happens in this tab. github.com/seantalts/stanli · npm · PyPI

This page reports a shifted lp__

The browser runtime is built with STANLI_LITE_LP, which drops stan-math's propto instantiations to halve the download. Those are exactly the terms that do not depend on the parameters, so every gradient and every generated quantity here is bit-for-bit what CmdStan computes, and the posterior you sample from is the same one. What moves is lp__ itself: it sits a per-model constant above CmdStan's, so do not compare this page's lp__ against a CmdStan run, and do not use it for anything that reads log densities as absolute numbers (Bayes factors, marginal likelihoods, bridge sampling).

Because the sampler adds lp to the kinetic energy, a pinned seed also draws a different chain here than in the wheels. It is an equally valid chain from the same posterior, not different math.

The PyPI wheels ship the exact build and match CmdStan's lp__. Details in docs/lite-lp.md.
